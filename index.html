Line by Line

Learning a language from films and shows, one line at a time.

Line by Line is a personal study app for the Mac and an Android phone. You watch with subtitles, look up and save words and phrases from each line, drill them, and send them to Anki.

Your study data is kept as files on your own devices. If you connect Google Drive, the app also keeps a copy in a folder of your Drive called Line by Line, which is how your devices stay in step.

It's made for one person's own use and isn't offered to the public.
