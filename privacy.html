Privacy policy

Line by Line · updated 28 October 2026

What the app can see in your Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, Line by Line asks for Google's drive.file permission. That lets it create files and use the files it created, in a folder called Line by Line in your Drive. It can't see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

What it keeps

Your study progress (the words you've saved, your notes, the lines you've watched, your study sessions and settings), and the subtitles, pictures and videos of the titles you choose to study on your phone: as files on your own devices, and, once you connect Google Drive, in that Line by Line folder. The copy in your Drive isn't encrypted by the app; Google keeps it as it keeps your other Drive files.

What never goes to Google Drive

Your sign-ins and your keys for other services. Each device keeps those to itself, in its own secure storage, and never sends them to Drive or to your other devices.

What it shares

Line by Line has no server of its own, and nothing is collected by the app's maker: there are no analytics, ads or tracking. Your devices sync through your own Google Drive, directly, and your phone can also sync with your Mac over your home network. On the Mac, the app works with Anki, on the same Mac.

Some tasks use online services you choose, under your own accounts: Google's and Anthropic's AI, to make subtitles and study notes from a title's audio and lines, and The Movie Database, for a title's details and pictures. Each service gets only what that task needs, and its own terms say what it may keep.

Removing access

You can remove Line by Line's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the Line by Line folder from your Drive.

Google API Services

Line by Line's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
